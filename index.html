The problem

AI was supposed to raise the ceiling.
For game audio — it lowered it.

Base audio models have no context for the game. Teams generate hundreds of takes, sift through them by hand, and settle for an average result far from what they imagined.

The standard is low and incomplete — we can’t accept that.

Three tiers

Three ways to build with AI audio.

Tier 1 · fragile

Wrapper

One external model behind an interface. Breaks when the model changes, fails, or ships the feature itself.

Tier 2 · b.unit today

Compound

Routes across base models. Post-trained on production data. Wired into the pipeline. Any model can be replaced.

Tier 3 · destination

Base model

Owns the full stack and the result. Built for what games need.

Our Unique Formula; Dynamic base model + post-training on our own data: we build a brain that always keeps context of the game.
